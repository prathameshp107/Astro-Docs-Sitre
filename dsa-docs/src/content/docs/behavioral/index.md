# Behavioral Interview Prep

Most candidates over-index on algorithms and then lose the round nobody practiced: **behavioral**. At Amazon the Leadership Principles rounds carry equal or greater weight than coding; at every company, the hiring-manager round is behavioral.

> **The insight:** great behavioral interviewing isn't "practice 500 questions." It's **build 8-10 great STAR stories, each mappable to 5+ question types**, then practice retrieving and adapting them under pressure.

## How to actually prepare

1. Read the [STAR method](./01-star-method) guide once.
2. Open the [Story Bank](/behavioral) and write 8-10 stories from your real experience — the builder nags you in real time if your Situation runs long, your Action is too thin, you say "we" more than "I", or your Result has no number.
3. Check the **Coverage Matrix** tab — it shows which question categories (conflict, failure, leadership, ambiguity, delivery, disagreement, growth, cross-team, customer focus, innovation) you can currently answer, and gives you memory-jogger prompts for the gaps.
4. Use the **Question Bank** to drill specific categories or company styles (Amazon LP-mapped, Google, Meta) and compare your answer against the rubric and the annotated weak-answer example.

The product isn't quantity of questions seen — it's having a small set of real stories you can adapt to almost anything asked.
