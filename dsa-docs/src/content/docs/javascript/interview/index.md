## 💡 Interview Tips

- **Explain your thought process** — interviewers want to know *how* you think
- **Start simple**, then add complexity (edge cases, performance, trade-offs)
- **Know the tradeoffs** — there's rarely one right answer
- **Practice coding** common utilities from scratch: `debounce`, `deepClone`, `Promise.all`
- **Understand the WHY** behind language features, not just the what
